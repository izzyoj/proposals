Buyer Experience Map

Sydney Residential Real Estate — 7 Stages, Trigger to Settlement

"I help Sydney real estate principals stop losing buyers between enquiry and offer, using AI to keep more of them engaged, so agents sell faster, for more, and win the next listing."

Who is the buyer?

A Sydney household with a real, life-event-triggered need to purchase. Not casual browsers. They've passed the scroll-and-daydream phase. They have skin in the game: a lease ending, a baby coming, an inheritance clearing, a job relocation confirmed. The life event is the ignition.

What triggered the search?

Not curiosity. A forcing function. The specific trigger defines their urgency and their tolerance for friction. A forced seller is fast. A lifestyle upgrader is slow. An investor is analytical. The agent who knows the trigger has an enormous advantage in every subsequent interaction.

Real alternatives

  • Keep renting (inertia, cheapest short-term)
  • Buy in a different suburb
  • Buy later (wait for prices or rates to shift)
  • Buy through a buyer's agent (the response to a poor agent experience)

Barriers between them and an offer

  • Finance uncertainty
  • Information asymmetry (agent knows everything)
  • Fear of overpaying
  • Inspection anxiety (hidden defects)
  • Silence from agents (the "maybe" buyer gets zero contact)
  • Loss aversion (buying wrong is more painful than missing right)

What "progress" looks like to them

Not finding the perfect property. Feeling like they're getting closer to certainty. Each agent touchpoint is an opportunity to either reduce uncertainty or increase it. AI makes deliberate uncertainty-reduction systematic and scalable.

The Gap
The Gap
No agent is present at this stage. The agent who gets called first is usually the one on the portal listing the buyer happened to find, not the one best positioned to serve them.
The Gap
Agents are absent during the buyer's research phase. The buyer forms opinions about suburbs, prices, and compromises entirely without agent input. When first contact happens, the agent has to undo as much as they reinforce.
The Gap
Average response time to portal enquiries in Sydney: 4 to 18 hours. Many buyers have already booked an inspection with a competing property by then. The initial response, when it comes, is often a generic "thanks for enquiring, here are the inspection times" with zero personalisation. Agents treat all enquiries as equal. The buyer who is 90-day ready gets the same auto-response as someone 12 months away.
The Gap
Agents run 5 to 10 open homes on a Saturday morning. The buyer gets a minute or two of face time. Post-inspection, most agents send no follow-up specific to that buyer's experience. The buyer leaves with questions they didn't ask. The agent has no record of what mattered to that specific buyer.
The Gap
40 to 60% of seriously interested buyers walk away at this stage. Agents treat it as the buyer's problem. "The B&P is their responsibility." Agents go quiet because there's nothing for them to do. The buyer experiences this silence as confirmation the agent doesn't care. A buyer receives a 60-page building report full of technical language. Three items flagged as "major defect." They panic. They call the agent. They get vague reassurance. They buy somewhere else.
The Gap
The gap is offer guidance and feedback loop speed. Buyers want to know: "Is my number in the right zone?" Most agents, under time pressure, give vague answers. Post-offer, the feedback loop is slow. "We'll get back to you" with no timeframe. The buyer is in limbo, unable to pursue other properties, unable to close.
The Gap
The post-exchange period is completely abandoned by most agents. The agent's economic incentive to stay engaged disappeared at exchange. Lost at two levels: the buyer never becomes a referral source, and the buyer never becomes a future vendor lead.
AI Opportunity
AI Opportunity
Passive pre-enquiry nurture. AI-powered suburb guides, market condition summaries, and "what can you buy for $X in Y suburb" tools on the agent's website convert passive browsers into identified leads before first contact.
AI Opportunity
AI-powered buyer qualification and suburb matchmaking. A "what are you looking for?" tool on the agent's website captures buyer criteria and returns a personalised suburb briefing. Positions the agent as a knowledgeable guide before the buyer has found a specific property.
AI Opportunity
Instant intelligent first response. Within 60 seconds of an enquiry: acknowledges the specific property, provides price guide, upcoming inspection times, and 2 to 3 comparable sales, asks a single qualifying question. The agent gets the reply drafted and a qualification score before they even see the notification. This is the highest-ROI AI intervention in the entire buyer journey.
AI Opportunity
Post-inspection debrief workflow. Within 2 hours of inspection close: AI-generated personalised follow-up with building report summary, strata levy detail, comparable sales table, and recommended B&P inspector. Also: AI inspection sign-in app captures buyer details, property interest rating, and buying timeline at the door. Automatic attendee data, not a clipboard and pen.
AI Opportunity
Three interventions: (1) B&P interpretation summary, buyer uploads report and AI returns a plain-language read on which defects are cosmetic vs structural, with estimated remediation cost range. (2) Proactive check-in at day 3 and day 7 post-inspection if no offer received: "Hey [name], I know you're working through the numbers. Can I answer anything?" (3) Finance confidence tool, integration with buyer's broker to confirm serviceability on this specific property at target price.
AI Opportunity
Comparable sales dashboard delivered in real time: 5 most comparable sales in the last 90 days with price-per-sqm analysis and spread between guide and actual sale price on this agent's last 10 campaigns. For private treaty: AI-generated offer submission workflow with timestamps and automated buyer acknowledgement. For auction: AI-prepared comparable sales brief delivered 48 hours before auction.
AI Opportunity
Automated settlement support sequence. After exchange, an AI-generated week-by-week checklist: what to do this week, who to call, what documents are due. Low-effort for the agent, extremely high-value for the buyer. Post-settlement: AI-triggered check-ins at 30 days, 90 days, and 12 months. This is the relationship pipeline that feeds both referrals and future listings.
