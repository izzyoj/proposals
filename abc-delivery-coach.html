01 · Playback

The unit of improvement isn’t the sprint. It’s the system around the team.

The brief connects coaching, facilitation, training, impediment removal, stakeholder relationships, product thinking and continuous improvement. That points to a role bigger than ceremony management.

PeopleCoach individuals and grow capability
TeamBuild autonomy and stronger ways of working
SystemRemove friction, dependencies and impediments
ValueConnect delivery to product and strategic outcomes

Fixing ceremonies without addressing decision-making, dependencies, capability or product clarity just makes dysfunction run on a two-week cadence.

02 · How I coach

Diagnose before prescribing.

I’m framework-literate, not framework-led. Scrum, Kanban, SAFe, Dual Track and Lean are tools. The starting point is understanding what is actually stopping the team from delivering value.

Listen

Understand the people, pressures and context before changing the process.

Observe

Look at flow, behaviours, hand-offs, decision points and where work gets stuck.

Experiment

Make the smallest useful intervention and test whether it improves the system.

Embed

Transfer capability until the team can recognise and solve the problem themselves.

04 · Autonomy

A good coach builds capability, not dependency.

The destination is not permanent Agile supervision. It is a team that can recognise problems, have the difficult conversations and improve its own system.

Coach-ledTeach, demonstrate and create enough structure to make the problem visible.
Coach-supportedFacilitate, question and help the team make its own decisions.
Team-ownedObserve, challenge when useful, and let the team own continuous improvement.

Autonomy without clarity becomes chaos. Clarity without autonomy becomes control. Coaching helps teams build both.

07 · Pragmatism

The framework should adapt to reality. Not the other way around.

At DISR, fragmented work, weak backlog readiness, unclear milestones and tightly sequenced technical dependencies meant the answer wasn’t simply “more Scrum”.

Framework theatre

Force every problem through the same ceremonies. Optimise compliance with the method. Call it Agile because the calendar says Sprint.

Context-led coaching

Strengthen backlog readiness, integrate release planning, make dependencies visible and adapt the operating model to the work that actually exists.

Scrum when Scrum helps. Kanban when flow matters. Hybrid when reality demands it. Evidence always.

08 · Culture

Psychological safety and accountability aren’t opposites.

High-performing teams need enough safety to surface problems early and enough ownership to do something about them.
