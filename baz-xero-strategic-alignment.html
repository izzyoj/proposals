04 · The operating model

From pilots to an investable portfolio.

One coherent way to evaluate, sponsor, deliver and sustain AI-enabled change.

Opportunity intake

Start with business friction

Capture problem, baseline, process owner, users, data dependencies and the cost of doing nothing.

Commercial prioritisation

Make choices visible

Compare expected value, effort, confidence, risk, feasibility and time to value, including non-AI alternatives.

Test and validation

Prove the hypothesis

Use small, bounded experiments with success measures, human checks, guardrails and stop conditions.

Adoption and ownership

Design beyond launch

Agree process changes, training, operational ownership, exception handling and benefit measurement before scaling.

A proposed framework for discussion, not a diagnosis of Xero's existing practices.

05 · Decision quality

From exciting demos to sensible investments.

The questions I'd insist on answering before committing serious money or organisational energy.

What is the baseline?

Minutes, errors, rework, hand-offs, spend or customer impact before the proposed change.

Why AI?

Would better workflow design, basic automation, existing SaaS or clearer policy solve it more simply?

What does success cost?

Implementation, integration, inference, assurance, training, ongoing support and change overhead.

Who owns the realised benefit?

Accountability after launch, adoption target, actual savings and what gets retired or redesigned.

AI isn't the outcome. Better economics, better experiences and better decisions are.

07 · First 90 days

Learn fast. Prove one thing. Build for repeatability.

An indicative approach, to be adapted after understanding Xero's priorities, team and constraints.

Days 01 to 30 · Diagnose

Understand where value leaks

Listen across business and technology. Map candidate workflows, existing initiatives, decision rights and evidence. Establish baseline economics and a prioritisation lens.

Days 31 to 60 · Validate

Test the strongest opportunities

Size benefits and full costs. Align sponsors, security, data and process owners. Run a bounded experiment against agreed success and stop criteria.

Days 61 to 90 · Embed

Turn proof into an operating decision

Decide scale, redesign or stop. Build adoption and ownership into the workflow. Agree how realised benefits and exceptions will be monitored.

The enduring output

A repeatable decision system

A shared way to discover, fund, validate, operationalise and measure AI opportunities rather than treating every request as a standalone experiment.

The 90-day goal isn't a pile of pilots. It's evidence, a decision and a repeatable way to create value.
